LET’S PLAY WITH WORDS

Small parts.
New meanings.

  1. Choose roots or word parts. Drag or tap up to three into the middle. For example: auto + bio + graphy.
  2. Add a prefix or suffix. You can use more than one.
  3. Predict the meaning. Say it to a partner.
  4. Check your word. Read the meaning and try changing one part.

Not every combination makes a word. Some spellings change: vise drops its e in revising, and biography becomes biographies.

Two tiles say in- because they have different meanings. vis, vise and vid(e) are forms of a root meaning “to see”.

This playground uses the word parts marked Unit 2 in the Level 1 morpheme list, plus earlier affixes needed to build them. The word collection is curated; an unlisted combination might still be a word. Ask your teacher.

For measurements, milli means one thousandth and micro means one millionth.
