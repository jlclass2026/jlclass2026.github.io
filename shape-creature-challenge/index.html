BETTER TOGETHER

Build. Describe. Surprise.

1
Make a secret creature.

The designer builds while their partner looks away. Start with about 6–12 shapes.

2
Use your words.

Hide the original and pass the laptop. The designer describes each shape, colour, size and position. The partner listens, asks questions and rebuilds.

3
Reveal and compare.

Look at both creatures. Which directions helped? What could be clearer? Celebrate the surprises, then swap jobs.

Two laptops? Choose “We have two laptops” on each device. One person makes the secret original; the other rebuilds on their own screen. Keep screens apart, then compare them in person.

Need a reminder? On one laptop, “Designer: check original” shows the original only after a look-away warning. Hide it again before returning the laptop.

Controls: Tap to add; drag to move. Or select a shape and tap a blank spot. Arrow keys move; Shift makes bigger steps. Delete removes. Ctrl/Command + Z undoes. Circles, squares and regular polygons keep their proper proportions.
