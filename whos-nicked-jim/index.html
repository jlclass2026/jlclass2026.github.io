THE VERY SHORT VERSION

Hide. Roll. Solve. Search.

  1. Take turns hiding your four characters. The other player looks away.
  2. Roll the dice and move clockwise around the outside.
  3. Solve your space: make 20, or double a number twice.
  4. A correct answer opens one of your opponent’s ten hideouts. A miss shows a handy strategy before the next turn.

Find all four first to win. If time runs out, finish the pair of turns so you each get the same number. Most captures wins; equal captures is a draw.

Take your time with each question. Use fingers, counters, paper or a teacher’s help. There’s no individual question timer. The round clock pauses while this help is open.
