What does this model mean?

This is a guided, illustrative model, not a real-world farm audit or a grade. Extra support systems have been added so that each showcased farm has a sustainable pathway through all four seasons. The drawings do not supply real measurements, crop varieties, local weather or running costs.

Water figures use invented model units, not litres. Each season adds collected water and planned reserve supply, subtracts farm use, and caps storage at 100 units. Overflow is routed to a planted soak area. Other systems are shown as care plans, not measured yields or energy calculations. Seasons follow autumn → winter → spring → summer; exact crop calendars depend on location and variety.

Seasonal scenes and added buildings are imaginative interpretations. The “From the design” notes identify the ideas in each original plan; the “Added for this year cycle” notes identify extensions.
