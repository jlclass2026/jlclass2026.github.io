Food and fibre

    About these living scenes

    Each scene reimagines the layout, colours and farming ideas in a class design with photorealistic artwork. Weather, water and the production journeys are animated in 2.5D; the landscape is not a fully modelled 3D farm. Seasonal weather is illustrative, not a local forecast, and the artwork shows the design’s crops together rather than their exact harvest dates.

    The cycles explain ways to care for a farm. They do not measure real-world survival or assess anyone’s work. Original worksheets and names are not published.
