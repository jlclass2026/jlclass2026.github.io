    How to play

    1. Take turns placing two predictions in different chambers. You share one pair of dice.
    2. Reveal the first die. Once per game, a player may rescue one prediction if it can no longer happen. A rescued hit earns one point.
    3. Reveal the second die. Each player independently enters the sum, larger minus smaller, and product. Patterns are checked automatically.
    4. Each matching prediction earns the points shown on its tile (one if rescued). Play four rounds and compare totals.

    Dice Lab

    Two fair dice have 36 equally likely ordered outcomes. Row = first die; column = second die. 2 then 5 and 5 then 2 count separately.
