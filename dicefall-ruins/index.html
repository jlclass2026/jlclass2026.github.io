How to play

  1. Take turns choosing two exact numbers on the board. You can pick in any operation. Both players can choose the same number.
  2. Roll the two shared dice. Together, enter the sum, the larger number minus the smaller, and the product.
  3. Every matching pick earns one point. Play four rounds. The highest score wins.

Dice Lab

There are 36 equally likely ordered outcomes: the row is the first die, the column is the second. A 2 then a 5 is different from a 5 then a 2.
